# Throughout the Semester

Beyond the midterm exam, most of your grade in this course comes from presentations delivered at several
points across the semester &mdash; two rounds of SOTA paper presentations, and three milestones on your final
project. This page summarizes how many presentations you'll give, when, and how each contributes to your final
grade. For full formatting and grading details, see [Important Information]({{ site.baseurl }}/important-information/);
for exact dates, see [Course Content]({{ site.baseurl }}/course-content/).

## Presentations at a Glance

| Category | Occasions | When | Format | Contribution to Grade |
|:---------|:----------|:-----|:-------|:-----------------------|
| SOTA Paper Presentation | 2 rounds | Week 5 & Week 11 | Pairs; 10 min total (&asymp;8 min presenting + Q&amp;A) | 30% of course grade (15 marks/round) |
| Final Project &mdash; Overview Presentation | 1 | Week 7 | Team; topic, novelty, plan, feasibility | 5 of 40 Final Project points |
| Final Project &mdash; Update Presentation | 1 | Week 10 | Team; progress, implementation updates, timeline | 5 of 40 Final Project points |
| Final Project &mdash; Final Presentation & Submission | 1 (across 2 class days) | Weeks 15&ndash;16 | Team; full deliverable presented and submitted | Remaining 30 of 40 Final Project points |

That's **5 presentation occasions** in total: 2 as part of the SOTA paper rounds, and 3 as part of the final
project's milestones.

## How This Adds Up to Your Final Grade

| Component | Weight |
|:----------|:-------|
| Participation & Quizzes | 10% |
| Midterm Exam | 20% |
| SOTA Paper Presentations (2 rounds) | 30% |
| Final Project (overview + update + final) | 40% |

Presentations alone &mdash; SOTA papers plus all three final project milestones &mdash; make up **70% of your
final grade**, so consistent preparation and attendance across the semester matters more than any single
exam. See [Assessment Breakdown]({{ site.baseurl }}/assessment-breakdown/) for the full grading weights.
