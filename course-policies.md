# Course Policies

- There will be no make-ups or late work accepted.
- There may be extra credit assignments given.
- **Cheating and plagiarism are strictly prohibited.** All work submitted for grading must be your own. For coding projects, this means you may not copy code found on the web and submit it as your own — the point of the coding projects is to take the time to code the machine learning algorithms and reproduce result figures from scratch. It is OK to discuss intellectual aspects with other students, but it is NOT OK to copy from other students or other sources. Academic integrity violations will result in penalties including, but not limited to, a zero on the assignment, a failing grade in the class, or expulsion from NAU.
- **Electronic devices** must support learning in the class. Cell phones, PDAs, music players, and other entertainment devices must be off (or in silent mode) during lecture and may not be used at any time. Laptops are allowed for note-taking and in-class activities only — no web surfing or other use.
- Grades will be entered in BBLearn but your official final grade is calculated using the scale above and entered in LOUIE. Your final course grade will not necessarily appear in BBLearn — check LOUIE for your final grade.
- **Email to the instructor** must be respectful and professional: complete sentences and correct grammar, full context for your question, and a subject line prefixed **"CS472/572"**. Allow up to three business days for a response; for longer discussions or several questions, come to office hours instead.
- Visiting the instructor during office hours is encouraged! Feel free to talk about the class, careers, research, or topics related (even loosely) to the course.
- Anonymous feedback is welcome any time via the "parking lot" — put a sticky note on the door as you leave the classroom.

Full course policies and university policy statements (academic integrity, AI use, accessibility, Title IX, and more) are reproduced on the [Syllabus Policy Statements]({{ site.baseurl }}/syllabus-policy-statements/) page, and in the [syllabus document]({{ site.baseurl }}/Fall_2026/CS%20475-572-Fall2026-Unsupervised-Learning-Syllabus.docx).
