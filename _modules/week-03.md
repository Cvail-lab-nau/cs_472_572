title: 'Week 3: Clustering II — GMMs & the EM Algorithm'
---

Gaussian mixture models and the Expectation-Maximization algorithm. This week introduces probabilistic (soft)
clustering via Gaussian Mixture Models, framing clustering as a density-estimation problem rather than a
hard-assignment one. It covers the Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm used to fit GMM parameters
(component means, covariances, and mixing weights) through iterative E-steps and M-steps, and contrasts GMM's
soft, probabilistic cluster assignments with k-means' hard assignments.
