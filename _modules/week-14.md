title: 'Week 14: Self-Supervised Learning & Emerging Methods'
---

Self-supervised learning methods and their relationship to the representation learning covered earlier in the
semester, followed by emerging methods in deep generative learning. The emerging-methods portion introduces
diffusion models &mdash; the forward (noising) and reverse (denoising) processes that define how they learn to
generate data &mdash; and situates them relative to other generative methods, such as autoencoders and GANs.
