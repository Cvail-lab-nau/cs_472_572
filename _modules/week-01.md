---
title: 'Week 1: Introduction & Applications'
---

Course overview, motivation for unsupervised and self-supervised learning, and applications of discovering
structure in unlabeled data. This week focuses on framing the difference between supervised and unsupervised
learning, why labeled data is often unavailable or expensive, and a tour of real-world use cases &mdash;
clustering for customer/market segmentation, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction for visualization, and
self-supervised pretraining &mdash; that motivate the methods covered throughout the semester.

Before Week 2, review the Python/NumPy/Pandas basics; before Week 4, review the linear algebra & statistics
you'll need for clustering and representation learning. Any comparable refresher material is fine as long as
you're comfortable with the concepts.
