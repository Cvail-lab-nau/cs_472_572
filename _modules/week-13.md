**Grading Note:** This presentation will carry 5 of your total grade (5 out of 40 marks allocated to the
"Final project update Presentation").

**What Your Presentation Must Include:**

- **Clear Problem Setup & Direction**
  - Be confident and specific about your project scope.
  - Clearly state the dataset(s) you are using.
  - Clearly describe the methods/models you plan to implement.
- **Incorporation of Feedback**
  - Include a dedicated slide summarizing:
    - Key comments/feedback received during your first project presentation.
    - How you are addressing or have already addressed those comments.
- **Progress So Far (Concrete Evidence)**
  - Clearly show what has been completed:
    - Data collection and preprocessing steps.
    - Any initial implementations.
  - Provide visual or qualitative evidence, such as:
    - Examples of your dataset (e.g., sample inputs/outputs).
    - Description of data quality, structure, or challenges.
  - If applicable, include preliminary results (even if small or incomplete).
- **Implementation Updates**
  - Clearly state:
    - What has already been implemented.
    - What insights or observations you have gained so far.
- **Timeline & Planning**
  - Include a timeline of completed work (what you have done so far).
  - Include a forward-looking timeline outlining:
    - What remains to be done.
    - How you plan to complete the project before the final deadline.
- **Clarity & Confidence**
  - Your presentation should reflect that you have a clear and structured plan.
  - Avoid vague descriptions, be specific about your approach and progress.

This update presentation is intended to ensure that your project is on track and grounded in actual progress.
Make sure your slides clearly communicate both what you have done and what you will do next.
