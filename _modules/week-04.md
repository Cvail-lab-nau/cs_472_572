First round of student group presentations on state-of-the-art (SOTA) research papers.

- [Presentation Rubric (Undergrad)]({{ site.baseurl }}/Spring_2026/Files/CS%20472_572_%20Paper%20presentation%20Rubric_%20UnderGrad.docx)
- [Paper Sign-Up Sheet]({{ site.baseurl }}/Spring_2026/Files/CS%20472_572%20Group_%20Paper.xlsx)

**Format:** groups of two present for 10 minutes total &mdash; about 8 minutes presenting, followed by Q&A.
Presentations run across two class sessions.

**Grading (15 marks per round, worth 30% of the course grade combined with Week 12):**

| Component | Marks |
|:----------|:------|
| Attendance (both presentation days) | 2 |
| Peer evaluation | 5 |
| Instructor evaluation | 8 |
