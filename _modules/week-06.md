---
title: 'Week 6: Hierarchical & Density-Based Clustering'
---

Agglomerative/divisive hierarchical clustering, dendrograms, DBSCAN, and k-medoids. This week focuses on
hierarchical clustering &mdash; both agglomerative (bottom-up merging) and divisive (top-down splitting)
approaches &mdash; and how to read and cut a dendrogram to choose a number of clusters. It also introduces
density-based clustering with DBSCAN, which finds clusters of arbitrary shape and flags outliers as noise, and
k-medoids as a more outlier-robust alternative to k-means for non-Euclidean data.
